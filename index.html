Number Coding Hours and Window Hours in Metro Manila (2026)

Check the usual UVVRP schedule, plate-number coding days, daytime window hours, city differences, exemptions, and practical tips before you travel.

Coding hours in Manila generally follow the Unified Vehicular Volume Reduction Program (UVVRP). Under the commonly used Metro Manila schedule, restrictions apply during the morning session from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM and the evening session from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Friday, according to the last digit of a vehicle's plate number. The usual daytime window is 10:01 AM to 4:59 PM, when coded vehicles may generally travel on roads covered by the standard scheme.

Important: Rules can vary by city, road, vehicle class, and official suspension announcements. Makati is commonly reported to enforce a longer 7:00 AM–7:00 PM restriction without the standard window. Confirm the latest rules with the relevant local government or MMDA before driving.

What Are Window Hours in Metro Manila?

Window hours are the daytime period between the two standard coding sessions. Under the usual schedule, this is from 10:01 AM to 4:59 PM. The window is not universal: a city with a different local rule, such as Makati's commonly reported no-window schedule, may not allow the same travel period.

Which Vehicles Are Restricted During Coding Hours?

The scheme primarily affects private vehicles whose plate endings match the day’s schedule, on roads where the UVVRP is enforced. Restrictions are based on the vehicle’s plate number and applicable rules, not on the driver’s identity. Check whether your vehicle qualifies for a legal exemption before travelling during a restricted period.

Which Vehicles Are Exempt From Number Coding?

Exemptions depend on the current law and implementing rules. Categories often discussed include motorcycles, public utility vehicles, emergency vehicles, certain government vehicles, and qualifying electric or hybrid vehicles. Do not assume that a vehicle is exempt based only on its type or a general online list; confirm eligibility with the relevant authority.

Are electric and hybrid vehicles exempt?

The Electric Vehicle Industry Development Act (EVIDA, Republic Act No. 11697) provides coding-related exemptions for qualifying electric vehicles under applicable rules. Eligibility can depend on the vehicle's classification and current implementing guidance. Not every vehicle marketed as a hybrid necessarily qualifies, so confirm the exact model and registration classification with the appropriate authority.

How to Request a Number Coding Exemption

If you believe you qualify for a special exemption, contact the MMDA or the relevant local government first to confirm whether applications are accepted and what documents are required. A general preparation checklist may include:

  1. Prepare a formal request addressed to the relevant authority.
  2. Explain the reason for the request and identify the vehicle.
  3. Gather supporting documents and copies of the vehicle's OR/CR, if required.
  4. Submit the request through the official channel specified by the authority.
  5. Wait for written confirmation before relying on an exemption.
Remember: submitting a request does not automatically grant an exemption. Follow the current official process and wait for approval.

7 Practical Tips to Avoid a Coding Violation

1. Check your coding day the night before

Save a recurring reminder in your phone calendar. The commonly used schedule is Monday: 1 and 2; Tuesday: 3 and 4; Wednesday: 5 and 6; Thursday: 7 and 8; Friday: 9 and 0.

2. Plan non-urgent trips during the window

If your vehicle is coded that day, plan errands between 10:01 AM and 4:59 PM only where the standard window applies.

3. Consider travelling before 7 AM or after 8 PM

These are generally outside the standard restriction periods, but local schedules such as Makati's may differ.

4. Check official advisories on holidays and severe-weather days

Do not assume a suspension without confirmation. Check current MMDA and local-government announcements before leaving.

5. Plan your full route, not only the expressway segment

An expressway may be exempt while connecting city roads remain covered by coding. Check each part of your route.

6. Verify city-specific exceptions

Rules can differ by city and road. Confirm the exact route instead of relying on broad claims that an entire city is coding-free.

7. Confirm vehicle exemptions before relying on them

Check current law and official guidance for your vehicle's classification and registration. Do not assume that all hybrids or all special-purpose vehicles are exempt.

When Is Number Coding Suspended?

Regular coding is commonly not enforced on weekends, but public-holiday and special-event suspensions should be confirmed through official announcements. Weather alerts or a special event do not necessarily mean coding is automatically suspended.
